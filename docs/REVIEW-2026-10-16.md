## Reviewer's overall assessment

> The code quality is well above what the "not a developer by trade" framing suggests: the API is fast on the real 31k-channel catalog, the relay's SSRF guard and limits are thoughtfully done, writes are atomic, and the test suites are real and green. The launch risk is concentrated in the parts nobody has run end-to-end: the Docker images (never built), the EPG grabber at `EPG_SITES=all` scale, and the shared-volume permission race, any of which can turn a first boot into "nothing starts" or "no guide, ever". Beyond that, the two things I would not go live without are the broken takedown path (2.1) and permanent relay signatures (2.2). With 1.1–1.4 checked on the box and 2.1–2.5 fixed, this is a safe soft launch; the section-3 items are the ones that will page you in the first month.
